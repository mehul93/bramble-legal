Bramble

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Bramble is a gardening app that reminds you when to sow, prune and water. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. Bramble is made by Mehul Mandalia, who is responsible for your information under UK data protection law.

The short version

  • Your garden is kept on your phone and in your own iCloud account. Your plants, notes, photos and name are never sent to us.
  • Bramble doesn't collect usage data, track you or show ads, and we don't sell your information.
  • Your postcode is sent to Apple to find where you are, for local weather and to work out the sun in your garden.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Information on your phone and in your iCloud

Bramble doesn't have accounts. Everything you enter is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We can't see it:

  • your first name, postcode and town
  • your answers from setup, such as how you heard about Bramble and what you'd like help with
  • your plants, including any you add yourself, with their varieties, notes, planting dates, garden spots, care history and which ones you've starred
  • photos you add to plants, which you choose with the system photo picker (Bramble can't see the rest of your photo library)
  • your garden's direction, house size and garden length, and your settings

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Bramble keeps a copy of this information in your iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage. That's how your garden appears on another iPhone and comes back if you reinstall Bramble. Your units setting syncs too; other settings, such as appearance and notifications, stay on each device. Apple stores the copy in your iCloud account under the Apple Privacy Policy, and it counts towards your iCloud storage. We can't access it.

If you're not signed in to iCloud, or have turned iCloud off for Bramble, everything stays on your phone only. Your information is also included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you have them turned on.

Deleting it

Deleting Bramble removes the information from your phone. To delete the copy in iCloud as well, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud › Manage Storage › Bramble, and delete its data.

Information shared with Apple

Some features need Apple's services. Apple handles this information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

  • iCloud sync. If you use iCloud, your garden, profile and units setting are kept in your iCloud account, as described above.
  • Postcode lookup. Your postcode is sent to Apple Maps to find your town and approximate location.
  • Weather and frost alerts. Your approximate location, rounded to about 1km, is sent to Apple Weather for your local forecast.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store. We learn whether you have Premium, but never see your payment details.
  • Crash reports. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash reports and app statistics. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Bramble never asks for your phone's location. The compass used to find which way your garden faces works on your phone, and only the direction you save is kept.

Checking for new features

Bramble switches on new features by downloading a small settings file from our website, which is hosted by GitHub. The request doesn't include anything about you or your garden. Like any website, GitHub receives your device's IP address to deliver the file, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or store it.

If you contact us

If you email us, including through Help & feedback in the app, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, what you write, and the app and device details the app adds to the message. We use them only to reply and to fix problems you report, and delete the email once it's no longer needed to help you.

Why we use your information

  • To provide the features you use, such as local weather and sun mapping. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: running the app you've chosen to use.
  • To answer your emails. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: helping you with the app.
  • To provide Premium if you subscribe. Our lawful basis is performing our contract with you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit its use. The only information we hold is emails you've sent us. Everything else is on your devices and in your own iCloud account, where you can change or delete it yourself.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Bramble isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Bramble uses your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We'll tell you in the app about any significant change.
