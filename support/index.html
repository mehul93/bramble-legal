Help and support

Questions, problems or ideas for Bramble? Email mehul.h.mandalia@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from Mehul, who makes the app. In the app, Profile › Help & feedback has these answers and an email button that fills in your app version and device.

Premium and subscriptions

Restore Premium on a new phone

Open any Premium feature to see the upgrade screen, then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Bramble. Cancelling keeps Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

Redeem a code

On the upgrade screen, tap Redeem a code and enter it. After any free period, the subscription renews at the normal price unless you cancel.

Refunds

Purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders

Reminders aren't arriving

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Bramble, and that the reminder types you want are on in Bramble's Profile › Notifications. Reminders arrive at 8am on the day a task is due.

Your garden and data

Where is my garden stored?

On your phone. There's no account, so your plants, notes and photos aren't stored anywhere else. They're included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups if those are on.

Moving to a new phone

Restore your new iPhone from a backup of the old one and your garden comes with it.

Deleting your data

Deleting Bramble removes everything from your phone. Bramble doesn't collect usage data or keep copies of your garden anywhere else; the privacy policy has the details.
